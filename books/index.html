The AI Transformation Consultant

What Works, What Fails, and How to Lead the Engagement

A field guide for consultants who have to lead AI-transformation engagements and be interviewed on them. Part I teaches the language of AI in six short chapters, from classical machine learning to LLMs, agents, guardrails and regulation. The diagrams are crisp black-and-white illustrations and every claim is cited (332 sources). It was created with AI tools, and the book says so.

  • 10 chapters in two parts, each ending with concrete takeaways for the client room
  • Eight ways programmes fail and seven strategies that answer them, with cited evidence
  • Paired with a free 5-module YouTube companion course that follows the same structure

MONOGRAPH 04
July 2026 Paperback & Kindle Free Companion Course

Advanced GraphRAG & Knowledge Agents

A Friendly, Illustrated Guide to Graph-Based Retrieval

What comes after flat vector search? This illustrated guide moves from the structural blind spots of chunk-based vector RAG to the graph-based retrieval architectures that solve them, and the multi-hop knowledge agents built on top. Covers six major GraphRAG families across eight structured modules — including Microsoft GraphRAG, DRIFT, LightRAG, and HippoRAG.

  • Clear visual proof of why flat vector RAG breaks on global and multi-hop queries
  • Ontology extraction, hierarchical communities, Personalized PageRank, and DRIFT search
  • Written companion to the 19-module Advanced GraphRAG YouTube series

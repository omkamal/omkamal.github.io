AI for Business Consultants: A Technical Orientation

▶

A panoramic, technically literate view of AI for the consultant who has to lead the room. It moves quickly through classical machine learning, spends its weight on generative AI, agents, platforms and guardrails, then turns to what makes AI transformations succeed or fail. The last module scores two real cases against that framework and ends with an interview toolkit. Every figure on screen carries its source, and the vendor landscape is dated November 2026.

Who it's for: business and technology consultants, and anyone who knows software and finance but not machine learning, and has to challenge vendors and engineers, lead AI-transformation engagements or sit an interview on them. It follows the book The AI Transformation Consultant.

COURSE 02 · HANDS-ON PYTHON
Beginner → Intermediate 5 modules ~2.6 hours English + Egyptian Arabic October 2026

Reinforcement Learning for Python Developers with Stable-Baselines3

Train agents that learn by trial and error, with no heavy math. Each module teaches one idea with a memorable analogy, then runs short, real Stable-Baselines3 code and shows the measured result: a dog trainer for PPO, a restaurant critic's notebook for DQN, a steady hand for SAC and TD3. One project, a CoffeeShop that orders beans, grows through all five modules, and the last module ships it. 34 runnable examples, every number on screen comes from a real run.

Who it's for: Python developers who know classes and pip and want to use reinforcement learning. No calculus, no statistics. The course is available in English and in Egyptian Arabic (Arabic narration, English terms, code and on-screen text).

Advanced GraphRAG & Knowledge Agents

▶

A friendly, in-depth course on building structured, graph-based retrieval systems, from first principles to production multi-agent pipelines. It starts with why plain vector RAG hits a ceiling, then walks through the major GraphRAG families: ontology-driven graph construction, Microsoft GraphRAG and DRIFT, LightRAG, HippoRAG, LinearRAG, AGRAG and StructRAG, finishing with multi-tiered architectures and benchmarks.

Who it's for: Engineers and data scientists who already know basic RAG and want to go deeper into graphs.

Advanced GraphRAG & Knowledge Agents — Part 2

▶

The sequel to Part 1 covers the next five families of graph retrieval. It begins with LazyGraphRAG, which defers the cost of indexing, then spends a long module on temporal and agent-memory graphs (two clocks, validity intervals, contradiction handling, Graphiti and Zep, and the wider memory-system landscape). It closes with learned graph retrievers, hypergraphs and agentic graph traversal.

Who it's for: Anyone who finished Part 1, or who is building agents that need memory that changes over time.

COURSE 06 · DEEP RESEARCH TOUR
Beginner → Advanced 16 modules ~4.0 hours October 2026

Context Management & Agent Memory in LLMs

▶

Why does your AI assistant forget? This gentle, visual course starts with the basics (what context is and why models forget), then moves slowly through recent research on how agents manage context and memory: just-in-time retrieval, learned summaries, pruning, folding, memory editing, evolving playbooks, memory operating systems, graph memory and memory beyond text. Animated diagrams, slow and clear explanations, and real code demos run on local models.

Who it's for: Anyone building or using LLM agents who wants to understand context windows, long-task memory and the newest research, with no prior knowledge needed for the first two modules.
